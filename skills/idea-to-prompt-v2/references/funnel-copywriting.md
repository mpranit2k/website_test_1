# Funnel & Copywriting Architecture

## Hero Formulas
- `[Product] helps [specific audience] [achieve specific outcome] by [core mechanism].`
- `Stop [pain] and start [desired outcome] in [timeframe].`

## Value Hook Templates
- `"Designed for [role] at [company type] who need to [job to be done] without [common hurdle]."`

## CTA Microcopy
- Pattern: "Get [Outcome]" -> `"Get your animated hero."`
- Subtext: `"No credit card required"`, `"Takes under 3 minutes."`

## Testimonials & Stats
- Social Proof Bar: `"[X]+ teams [key result], [Y]% faster [outcome]."`
- Testimonial: `[Job title] at [company] + Avatar + Outcome-focused quote`.
